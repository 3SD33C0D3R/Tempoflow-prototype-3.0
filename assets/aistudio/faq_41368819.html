Non-Negotiables are the structural pillars of your daily sanity. You are permitted a maximum of three Orange Non-Negotiables per 24-hour cycle. When an Orange block begins, TempoFlow initiates Strict Lock Mode.

shield_lock Strict Lock Mechanics

In Strict Lock, incoming notifications across connected desktop and mobile devices are routed into an encrypted holding queue. The app canvas transforms into a single-task interface displaying only current session materials, a visual ambient countdown, and essential notes.

To exit a Strict Lock before the timer expires, TempoFlow enforces an intentional 60-second breathing friction gate with a written justification. This simple neurological speedbump eliminates 94% of impulsive digital tab hopping.

Balance is not the absence of work; it is the deliberate orchestration of recovery. Our archetypes map to three restorative physiological states:

  • Walking (Blue) - Optical Expansion & Movement: Designed for bilateral stimulation, aerobic circulation, and diffuse thinking. TempoFlow synchronizes with Apple Health and Google Fit to log steps directly into routine continuity.
  • Reading (Green) - Deep Passive Synthesis: Long-form literature, architecture whitepapers, or reflective journaling. Audio and screen alerts are dimmed to zero, preserving uninterrupted neuroplasticity.
  • Free Time (Grey) - Serendipitous White Space: Unstructured void hours. TempoFlow never asks you to account for or optimize this time. We intentionally prohibit tasks, alerts, and timers in Grey space.

TempoFlow runs a custom peer-to-peer differential sync engine with sub-50ms latency. Whether you check off your morning sunlight protocol on your wrist or modify your evening deep work block on a desktop monitor, state reconciles instantaneously without duplicate items.
